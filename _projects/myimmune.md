**What we build**
-----
[MyImmune](https://www.myimmune.ai/) reads the adaptive immune system. Every person carries a unique repertoire of T-cell and B-cell receptors, and that repertoire records what the immune system has seen and how it is responding. We sequence those receptors from a small blood sample and use machine learning to turn them into clinically useful signals.

Three product lines run on the same platform:

* **Biomarker development**: converting immune repertoire data into diagnostic biomarkers and clinical endpoints for oncology, infectious disease, and autoimmunity.
* **Response prediction**: stratifying patients by their likely response to cancer and autoimmune therapies to de-risk clinical trials.
* **AbSapien**: in silico antibody discovery that retrieves fully natural human antibodies from healthy repertoires instead of engineering them from animal-derived sources, cutting hit-to-lead time and downstream immunogenicity risk.

**My role**
-----
I co-founded the company in June 2024 with Prof. Daron Standley (Research Institute for Microbial Diseases, Osaka University) and Dr. Florian Hauer. As CTO I built the AI and engineering stack, including the FACTS classifier that reaches a mean ROC AUC of 0.99 across 16 disease classes from 81 million receptor sequences. Since June 2026 I lead the company as CEO.
