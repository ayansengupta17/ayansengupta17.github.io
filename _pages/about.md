2024 - Present
--------
Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of [MyImmune](https://www.myimmune.ai/), an Osaka University spin-out building an AI platform that deciphers the adaptive immune system from a single blood sample. I co-founded the company in June 2024 with Prof. Daron Standley (Osaka University) and Dr. Florian Hauer, served as CTO building the core AI and engineering stack, and took over as CEO in June 2026.

MyImmune translates adaptive immune receptor repertoires (T-cell and B-cell receptor sequences) into biomarkers for diagnostics, predicts patient responses to cancer and autoimmune therapies, and runs [AbSapien](https://www.myimmune.ai/), an in silico antibody discovery platform that retrieves fully natural human antibodies instead of engineering them from animal-derived sources.
