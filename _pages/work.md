Interest
------
My passion is to bring machine learning to a practical level where it is a product that solves challenging problems for people.
My major interests are in the domain of machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and AI for medical diagnostics and therapeutic discovery.

(June 2024 - Present)
